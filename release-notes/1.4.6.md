ArcFlare 1.4.6: calls from agents ring in the voice of a local speech model, there are two kinds of call with a tone each, and Whisper writes down what you said.

## Changes you may notice

- **Two kinds of call, two tools, two tones.** `call_phone_check` is a routine call and respects mute and your hours. `call_phone_emergency` rings with its own tone, even when calls are muted or outside your hours. `call_phone` still works and means a check-in. Tools in the app has a tone for each, chosen with Android's ringtone picker. Until you choose one, the emergency tone is the phone's alarm sound.
- **The phone rings when the first line is ready.** With a speech model chosen, a call is "preparing" until the model has made the opening line, then rings, so the call opens in that voice instead of the phone's own. A slow model no longer loses to the phone's 10 second wait.
- **A voice made from a description.** The call voice can be Qwen3-TTS VoiceDesign with a written description and a language, so an agent can sound like a native English speaker.
- **A tone for a preset voice.** The call voice setting takes an optional `instruct` (a tone) on models that support it.
- **Whisper on the whole call.** After you hang up, Whisper large-v3 transcribes the recording on this computer. The text is kept in the call's `transcript.json` and is part of what the agent gets back; the agent waits for it.
- **The speech model loads when the hub starts,** so the first call does not pay the load.

## Fixes

- A speech model that fails to make a line is now logged by the hub instead of silently falling back to the phone's voice

## Not done, and not checked

- **Whisper does not replace the phone's live recognition.** It runs after the call. During the call the agent still gets the phone's own text.
- **Calls still do not ring a phone whose app is closed.** The phone rings only while the app is open and connected.
- A slow speech model (Qwen3-TTS 1.7B takes about a minute a line on an AMD iGPU) makes the phone ring later. It is not made faster.
- A scheduled call remembers its kind only until the hub restarts.
- The Android change (two tones) was built and published locally, but not installed or tried on a phone: the phone's app is signed with the release key that only GitHub holds.

Update with `arcflare update`, or look first with `arcflare update --check`.
