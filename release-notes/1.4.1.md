ArcFlare 1.4.1: fixes for calls from agents to your phone, and for the phone page's call, question and chat screens.

## Read this first

- **Calls still do not ring a locked or backgrounded phone.** The phone rings only while the ArcFlare app is open and connected. Android silences the microphone of a background app and freezes its connection. Android apps 1.0.472709 add a call service that keeps the microphone on once you answer and a full-screen ring, but nothing here wakes a frozen or killed app.
- **Some of these fixes are in the Android app, not here.** The phone page (what you see in the app) is served by your computer, so its fixes arrive with this update. The fixes inside the Android app itself (marked "Android app" below) are in the Android apps 1.0.472709, published on arcflare.net after this release. The Android build passed in GitHub Actions; nothing was run on a phone.

## Changes you may notice

- Only owner accounts can see an agent's call, hear it ring, or see the questions agents ask the phone. Other accounts now get no call and no questions.
- `hang_up` now returns within about 25 seconds if the phone's recordings stop arriving, instead of waiting up to four minutes.

## Fixes

- Only owner accounts see an agent's call and hear it ring
- An agent's hang_up returns soon when the phone's recordings stop arriving
- A call the agent ended says the agent hung up, not the user
- A scheduled call is no longer deleted by later calls before it rings
- A call no longer gets stuck on "Listening" when the phone's recogniser goes quiet
- Hanging up while the phone is still speaking no longer leaves the call half-open
- A call whose microphone fails stops recording instead of running the phone's CPU flat out
- Each spoken answer in a saved call keeps its own recording, also after a listen that heard nothing
- A call keeps recording after a listen whose recogniser stopped reading the audio
- A failed or replaced app update download no longer spoils the update started after it
- A model with a chat template the phone cannot read is refused instead of crashing the app
- Calls: tapping Decline right after Answer no longer ends the call you answered
- Calls: an answer that lands after the agent hung up no longer reopens the call
- Calls: words heard after a call ended are no longer sent in the next call
- Calls: hanging up is tried again when the connection drops for a moment
- Calls: a missed call is no longer notified twice when two checks run at once
- Calls: the call screen catches up after the connection drops, so calls no longer go unseen
- Calls: an approval or the Back gesture during a call no longer takes away the call controls
- Calls: signing out during a call no longer hides that call or later ones
- Questions from agents: answering one no longer makes the next one disappear
- Questions from agents come back after a call or another sheet covered them
- The agent list no longer goes back to an older state when replies arrive out of order
- Replies that land after you open another agent no longer change that agent's screen
- Chats: a message that fails after you open another chat no longer lands in that chat
- Chats: Delete works again after a delete that failed
- App update: if the install setting cannot be opened, the update card says why
- Phone AI: Stop during a question that uses files ends the whole answer
- Phone AI: tool calls beyond the per-answer limit get a result, so the model is not confused
- Phone AI: a failed check of the phone no longer says the phone is unsupported
- A hang-up just before the next call starts no longer mixes up that call's answer files
- A listen whose answer file cannot be created no longer leaves an open pipe behind
- Hanging up a call now stops the agent's spoken line instead of letting it play on
- Closing the app during a call lets go of the speech recogniser and stops the agent's line
- A model that fails to load in an unusual way is refused instead of closing the app
- Agents' questions to the phone are shown to owner accounts only
- Calls: hanging up during the agent's goodbye line tells the agent you hung up

## Android app (in the apps 1.0.472709)

These are the fixes above that live in the Android app: the stuck "Listening", the open pipe after a failed listen, recording after a recogniser stops reading, the microphone failure that spun the CPU, hang-up stopping the spoken line, letting go of the recogniser when the app closes, the answer-file names after a silent listen, the update download that spoiled a newer one, and the chat template and model-load crashes.

## Not done, and not checked

- Nothing here ran on a phone. The phone page fixes have browser tests that fail without the fix, except the install-settings message and the 40 second Listen guard.
- Known and not fixed: if a hang-up request or an audio upload never answers, the call screen can still wait on "Saving" in rare cases; typing an answer while the phone is listening can play the agent's next line over the microphone.

Update with `arcflare update`, or look first with `arcflare update --check`.
