ArcFlare 1.4.5: the AI on the phone gets a real chat screen and is the first thing ArcFlare Agent opens.

## Changes you may notice

- **The AI on the phone has a chat screen.** Answer bubbles, a header with the model name and how full its memory is, a composer pinned at the bottom with a send and stop button, a writing indicator, and suggestions on an empty chat. New chat, Models and storage and Files are in a Chat options sheet. Answers are drawn as formatted text instead of raw `**` and code fences.
- **ArcFlare Agent opens on the AI.** In the Agent app the AI is the first tab, and the list of agents is one tap away under Agents. The AI runs on the phone, so it works before you sign in to the computer, and it asks the computer for no tools until you do.
- **Phone tools are a switch in Tools,** so the AI on the phone can ring and notify you when you want it to.
- **Phone tools used from the phone wait for your answer** and ring at most 3 times a minute.
- **Screenshots are allowed in the Android apps.** Before, screenshots, screen recording and the recent-apps picture were blank. If you want approvals, commands and pairing codes kept out of captures, turn off Allow screenshots in Tools.

## Fixes

- An empty chat no longer opens scrolled past its heading on a short screen
- On the pairing screen the chat keeps clear of the status and navigation bars
- The status dot stays on the first line when the model line wraps

## Not done, and not checked

- Nothing ran on a phone. The layout was checked in screenshots at 320x658 and 412x915 in light and dark, and the phone page flows passed 13 of 13 in Chromium.
- The Android Java change for screenshots was compiled against stubs and the Android 36 library. The Gradle build does not run in our cloud sessions, so the APK comes from CI.
- Allowing screenshots is the new default. It is a change in what the app protects unless you switch it back.

Update with `arcflare update`, or look first with `arcflare update --check`. The phone page is bundled inside the Android app, so these changes reach a phone with a new app build.
